Privacy Policy — Endo Things

Last updated: September 2026

Endo Things ("the app") is a symptom, cycle, and surgical history tracker built for people living with endometriosis and related conditions. Your privacy — especially around sensitive health information — is treated as the top priority in how this app is built.

The short version

Everything you log stays on your own device. Nothing you enter is uploaded, transmitted, sold, or shared with anyone — including the app's developer. There are no ads, no trackers, and no analytics reading your health data.

What data the app stores, and where

Endo Things stores all information locally, in storage tied to your device and your installation of the app:

  • Symptom, flare, and cycle entries you log on the calendar
  • Surgical history you add (types, dates, notes)
  • Profile details you choose to enter (name, pronouns, age, height, weight, allergies, conditions, profile photo)
  • App settings and preferences (theme, whether you menstruate, menopause status, app lock PIN if enabled)

None of this data ever leaves your device. There is no server, database, or cloud account behind this app — the developer has no ability to see, access, or recover your data, because it was never sent anywhere.

Backups

The app includes an optional manual backup feature. If you choose to use it, a file containing your data is created and saved directly by you, to a location you choose (such as your device's downloads). That file is only ever handled by you — the app does not automatically upload or sync it anywhere.

Profile photo

If you choose to add a profile photo, it is stored locally on your device the same way as everything else, and is never uploaded anywhere.

App lock (PIN)

If you enable the optional PIN lock feature, the PIN is stored locally on your device only, to gate access to the app itself. It is not recoverable remotely and is not transmitted anywhere.

Third-party services

The free web version of the app includes an optional, clearly-labeled external link to Ko-fi (a third-party donation platform) for people who wish to support the app's development. Tapping that link takes you to Ko-fi's own website, which has its own separate privacy policy. Endo Things does not receive any personal or payment information from that transaction.

If you purchase the optional one-time Premium unlock through the Google Play Store version of the app, that transaction is handled entirely by Google Play's billing system, governed by Google's own privacy policy and terms. Endo Things does not receive or store your payment details.

Children's privacy

Endo Things is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13. The app does not knowingly collect information from children, and since no data is collected or transmitted by the app at all, no such data is retained on any server.

Your control over your data

Because everything is stored locally, you are always in full control:

  • You can export a backup of your data at any time
  • You can delete individual entries, surgeries, or your whole profile within the app
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage/data through your device's app settings, permanently and completely deletes everything the app stored

Changes to this policy

If this policy is ever updated, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change to reflect that.
